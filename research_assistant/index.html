How this team works

LangGraph retrieves sources, runs two branches, joins their results, limits revisions, and pauses for approval.

AutoGen provides the researcher, benchmark analyst, and writer agents, using local Ollama.

LangChain/Ollama audits the draft against the original abstracts with structured findings and exact supporting quotes.

Collect → [Researcher + Analyst] → Writer → QA → Your approval. QA can request two revisions. An unresolved draft cannot be approved or exported.

What are the sources?

arXiv hosts research papers and preprints. We search it when you submit a topic and read the abstracts, not full papers. A listing does not guarantee peer review. No results means no report; the team does not invent replacement evidence.
